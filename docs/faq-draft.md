# Rentify help center: FAQ draft

> **DRAFT.** Content for the A3.4 help center (`app/help`). It describes the planned MVP, not shipped behavior. Anything marked **[TBD]** depends on a policy decision that is not recorded in `DECISIONS.md` yet. Do not publish a TBD answer until it is decided. Group headings match the accordion topics: Renting, Listing, Payments, Safety.

Terms used on the site: **Owner** (lists a device), **Renter** (rents a device), **Booking** (one reservation of a listing for a date range), **Deposit** (an amount held and released after a clean return), **Handover** and **Return** (the two moments the device changes hands).

## Renting

### How do I rent a device?

Browse or search for a device, pick your dates on the listing page, and send a booking request. The owner accepts or declines. If they accept, you pay, then arrange pickup in the booking chat.

### Why do I have to wait for the owner to accept?

Owners decide who borrows their devices. If an owner does not respond within **[TBD: 48 hours]**, the request expires and you are not charged.

### Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes, but what you get back depends on who cancels and how close the start date is. **[TBD: cancellation and refund rules by booking stage.]** You are not charged for a request the owner declines or that expires.

### How do I pick up and return the device?

At handover and again at return, you and the owner complete a short condition checklist and take photos in the app. Both of you confirm. The photos protect both sides if there is a disagreement about damage.

## Listing

### How do I list a device?

Choose **Lend**, select a category, and fill in the details for that category (for example battery cycles for a drone). Add clear photos, a daily price, a deposit and your pickup area. Publish when you are ready.

### Can I decline a request?

Yes. You can accept or decline each request. You are not required to give a reason.

### What happens if my device is damaged?

Take the handover and return photos, and confirm the return condition in the app. If you report damage, the deposit stays held while the issue is reviewed. **[TBD: who bears the loss in each damage and theft case, and how disputes are decided.]**

## Payments

### How does payment work?

After the owner accepts, you pay the rental fee and a deposit. **[TBD: payment gateway and accepted methods.]** Rentify does not store your card details. Payments go through a hosted payment form from the payment provider.

### What is the deposit?

A deposit is an amount held when you pay and released after the owner confirms the device came back in good condition. **[TBD: whether the deposit is a hold on your card or a charge that is later refunded, and for how long it can be held.]**

### When do I get my deposit back?

After the owner confirms a clean return. If the owner does not respond within **[TBD: 48 hours]** of the return, the booking may complete automatically. **[TBD: confirm this rule.]** If a booking is disputed, the deposit stays held until the dispute is resolved.

### Where can I see my payments?

Under **Bookings → Transactions**: rental fees, deposit holds, refunds and payouts, with dates and statuses.

## Safety

### How does Rentify protect owners and renters?

- Every booking has a chat that is only visible to the two people on the booking.
- Condition checklists with photos are recorded at handover and return.
- Reviews are two-sided and unlock only after a completed booking.
- You can report a listing, a user or a booking, and our team reviews reports.

### What items are not allowed?

See the **Prohibited items** page.

### How do I report a problem?

Use **Report** on a listing, a user profile or a booking. Describe what happened. Our team reviews reports and can hide listings or suspend accounts.

### Should I arrange payment or pickup outside Rentify?

No. Keep your booking, chat and payment on Rentify. Off-platform deals are not covered by deposits, evidence or dispute handling.

### How do you handle my personal data?

See the **Privacy** page. Short version: we collect only what is needed to run bookings, and we do not store card details.

### I can't use the site the way I need to. Who do I contact?

Use the feedback form on the Help page. We are working toward WCAG 2.1 AA for the main flows: keyboard use, screen-reader support, and enough contrast.
