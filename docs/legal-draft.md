- **Account data:** name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely hashed by our authentication provider), avatar.
- **Listing and booking data:** listing details and photos, booking dates, prices, status, and reviews.
- **Messages:** chat between the Owner and the Renter on a booking.
- **Condition records:** checklists and photos taken at handover and return.
- **Payment records:** payment status and references from the payment provider. We do not receive or store full card details.

## Prohibited items

> **Proposed list for review.** Edit with the team and legal or faculty review before publishing.

You may not list, offer or request any of the following on Rentify:

1. **Anything illegal** to own, rent or use in the Philippines, or that needs a licence or permission you do not have.
2. **Stolen, counterfeit or tampered devices**, including devices with removed serial numbers.
3. **Devices locked to someone else's account** or that you cannot lawfully rent out.
4. **Devices you do not own** or have no right to rent to others.
5. **Weapons and devices designed to harm**, intercept communications or break into other people's systems.
6. **Damaged or unsafe devices**, such as swollen or damaged batteries, exposed wiring, or devices that are a fire or shock hazard.
7. **Devices that hold personal data** that has not been fully wiped and reset before handover.
8. **Anything that breaks another person's rights**, such as pirated software or licensed content.
9. **Items that are not gadgets or electronic devices**, unless the team adds a category.
